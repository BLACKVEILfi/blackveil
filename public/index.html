06Transmissions

Asked on the temple steps.

Is BLACKVEIL a bank?

No. It is an immutable smart contract on Robinhood Chain that keeps USDG balances, handles, requests and agent vaults. Nobody — including us — can freeze or move what you hold in it.

Are my balances private?

Not yet. Today every amount is visible on chain, like any token transfer. Encrypted balances are planned and will be marked live only when the circuits and verifier are deployed and checkable.

What stops my agent from draining its vault?

The contract. agentPay() only moves funds when the amount fits the per-payment limit, the 24-hour limit and the vault. Above your review line it waits for you. Anything else is refused and logged.

What if the agent key leaks?

Pause or revoke the agent — revoking returns the vault to your balance in the same transaction. Or rotate to a new key and keep the vault, mandate and history.

What does $BLVE do?

It is the charter. Holding the on-chain minimum opens ten agent vaults instead of one and lets you claim three- and four-letter handles. It pays no yield: fees go to the house.

What can the owner key do?

Set the charter token once, set the charter minimum, choose where fees go, and hand over ownership. That is the full list — see Verify for the source.
